VideoCreator single-file distribution

The executable (Windows .exe or macOS binary) extracts its dependencies before starting. The extraction directory
contains the licenses shipped by .NET, FFmpeg and the font authors.
VideoCreator's own license is in LICENSE.

  Windows: BtbN static GPL build (latest master at build time; the FFmpeg
    commit is in "ffmpeg -version"). Builds and build scripts:
    https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  macOS (Apple Silicon): Martin Riedl static build of FFmpeg 9.0.2.
    Builds and build scripts: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/
    Source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n9.0.2
